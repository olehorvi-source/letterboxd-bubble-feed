2026-09-02 The Return (2003) thejoshl primary 4.0 no Been meaning to check this one out for a while but have to be in the right mood for that distinctly Russian bleakness, and this was as miserable and meditative a coming-of-age film as advertised. Occupies an interesting space between being hauntingly ambiguous (maybe even frustratingly, for some) about the details of its literal father-son mystery but ultimately so striking and tactile in terms of its bitter mood and gorgeous landscape photography that the larger symbolism at play here regarding authority and masculinity is all conveyed quite obviously and beautifully despite the ghostly, unresolved nature of the narrative. So sick to make a going fishing with dad road trip movie where the loving lesson at its center is that the world is cold, wet, harsh and lonely, and sometimes dads just disappear and you gotta move on and live with that.
