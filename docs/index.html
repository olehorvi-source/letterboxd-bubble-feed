2026-10-04 Flesh for Frankenstein (1973) thejoshl primary 4.0 no Gotta be in the running for most Italian film ever written and directed by an American. A huge part of that is Morrissey shooting this in Rome with a crew chock full of Italian greats: cinematographer Luigi Kuveiller ( Investigation of a Citizen Above Suspicion , A Lizard in a Woman’s Skin , Deep Red ), production designer Enrico Job ( Love and Anarchy , Seven Beauties ), and special effects artist Carlo Rambaldi ( Planet of the Vampires , A Bay of Blood , again Deep Red ) who provide the film with beautiful 70s lensing, colors, sets and gore. The symmetrical shots of the castle dining room table and mad science lab in particular are absolutely gorgeous. But more than just the technical, there's also the bizarre performances (and accent-heavy line deliveries) by Joe Dallesandro as the hunky, libidinous farm hand who keeps Monique van Vooren's Mrs. Frankenstein busy in the bedroom, and Udo Kier whose version of Dr. Frankenstein is the horniest and most deranged yet. Died at his delivery of "two girls... one man... he must be very powerful!" + "kidneys... gallbladder... liver!!!!" The dude quite literally can't cum without a hand full of bloody guts. Speaking of which, this Frankenstein is also most horrifically violent yet: graphic decapitations, disemboweling, dismemberment, necrophilia, etc... Commiting these kinky, disgusting atrocities in the name of achieving some sort of "master race" specimen. Despite all this, the overall tone often leans just as much campy softcore sex(ual liberation) comedy as it does elegant gothic horror, which is something I wasn't totally sold on the entire time but found really paid off by the end when Udo Kier was tragically monologuing through his own graphic penetration/impalement. After all, "to know death, you have to fuck life and gallbladder." That final corpse-riddled tableaux + the lyrical swelling score + the Frankenstein's creepy children inheriting/continuing his perverse experimentation (and how it echoes the eerie opening credits) ... Great stuff.
