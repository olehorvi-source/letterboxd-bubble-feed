But credit where it's due, the foundation of her being a child deliberately conceived in prison in order to inherit her mother’s trauma and raised/trained from birth to be a "demon" with the blade and complete her vendetta mission from the grave is simply one of the great 70s revenge movie set-ups, and doled out here with some effective non-linear storytelling. And lastly, this probably goes without saying: Meiko Kaji… That’s it. One of the greatest actresses to ever stare down the barrel of a camera. Feels like at any moment her eyes are going to pierce the screen and cut you up too for watching all her pain and suffering. Full discussion on ep 25 of my podcast SLEAZOIDS .
