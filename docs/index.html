2026-08-17 The Dark Knight Rises (2012) thejoshl primary 3.0 yes Easily the dumbest and clunkiest thing Nolan has put his name but also frequently a little awesome because of that. If there was a dictionary definition of a "bozo who kind of owns" movie this would be it. Completely ideologically incoherent (banks! cops! anarchy! occupy! fallen politicians! prisons! the people!) in a way that just feels a quaint and comical watching not as a teenager, but also in a way that feels like largely meaningless dress-up, a side effect of trying to give his trilogy closer a sense of grandness and ponderousness that matches the sheer scope of the imagery. It was strange to watch this in such close succession with Begins and The Dark Knight because they reveal how much less tight this is a conventionally functional movie but also how much more accomplished of a large-scale in-camera imagemaker he became during his time making these. The mid-air plane hijacking/kidnapping stunt, mid-film motorcycle chase, back breaking brawl (is it the only good hand-to-hand combat Nolan has ever filmed?), prison climb and cops vs. citizens/terrorists courthouse showdown are such a technical improvement over where he was with shooting and cutting action in 2005, and he makes very good use out of the budgetary increase for extras and locations. And I wanna respect that of all the movies this is the one he was willing to get a little goofy with it, but man is the writing just so janky, pacing so bizarre and the overall effect simply monotonous and exhausting after awhile. Even if I kinda like Bale in old man death wish Batman mode, and the Hardy Bane voice which is the among the funniest (complimentary) decisions of all time, and Hathaway who is soooo good at screwball femme fatale catwoman + has never looked better; as soon as Bruce fixes his spine with the power of belief to a bunch of fellow native inmates chanting what sounds like “this is awesome, awesome” I'm about ready for him to just wrap it up, and instead he's got a whole 40-minute nuclear bomb climax left that keeps cutting back to whatever JGL and Cotillard are up to. Very much enjoy that he had so much time to come up with an ending for his Batman and he went with "What if Alfred did Ben Affleck's Good Will Hunting line about the best part of his day?" [ 35mm ]
