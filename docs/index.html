2026-09-10 Hope (2026) thejoshl primary 3.0 no This review may contain spoilers. Legitimately bonkers Korean Extremity (both in terms of humor and gory violence) approximation of a Hollywood scifi-action blockbuster with a number of fluid and muscular monster movie setpieces among the best we've seen in some time, featuring some incredible wide-angle sweeping photography of its village, forest and mountain highway vistas, and some great constantly escalating/forward-momentum at all costs editing. Not only copying some of the scale and vocabulary of, but also loosely stringing its action around a plot, tone and character ensemble as nonsensicial as one of Michael Bay's later Transformers sequels. Appreciate how genuinely weird and gnarly this is, especially during the early stretch that has that Spielbergian patience and suspense before revealing the CG monstrosities, where we're often trailing just close enough to see the vast vehicle/building destruction and gruesomely torn/crushed corpses they've left in their wake a few seconds earlier. Things get a little wonkier when we actually see the PlayStation 3 Na'vi/ Attack on Titan mfrs in their full form (and their frankly hilariously serious, mocap performances by the top-billed western cast members, and their completely incomprehensible language/lore), but in a way I will confess to mostly finding charmingly bizarre. Sorta feels like what I imagine someone who's never played a videogame before might if dropped into one of the later Halo sequels as a starting point. I almost don't want the "Part 2" the ending teases because it would likely make all the shots of aliens talking to each other and doing teary-eyed stares you don't remotely understand less funny. Speaking of which, wasn't expecting this to be as gag-heavy as it was. Frequently amusing itself by letting its character indulge in a traditionally exciting slow-motion action hero movie beat ("Like a movie star! So hot!"), and then immediately comically punishing them in some sort of humiliating or gross fashion... Lost it at how long our beautiful badass leading lady has to listen to that old man diarrhea monologue for right after getting her Sarah Connor ass introduction, and the Hereditary payoff to how many times they dangle that dude trying to jump from horseback to the moving cop car. Unfortunately, some of the action gets a little too repetitive at this length, especially down the forest stretch (how many bullets and grenades these buggers can take starts to turn into a Wile E. Coyote bit at a certain point), and yet there's enough pure weird factor here for me to say anything but I had a good a time. Big fan of a movie just out of nowhere introducing a Prometheus ship or doing alien autopsy shit.
