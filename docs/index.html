2026-10-22 Other Mommy (2026) thejoshl primary 2.0 no This review may contain spoilers. Honestly, pretty slick and silly and delightfully, enjoyably stupid for much longer of its runtime than expected. I especially appreciated how quickly this allows the parents to witness and accept that there is in fact a literal live-action Coraline "Other Mother" monster trying to kidnap/consume their child into another dimension, even if it doesn't really know what else to do from there than a series of really poorly thought out (kind of amusingly so after awhile) runaway attempts. The surreal monster chase setpieces themselves were mostly effective if pretty derivative of Smile / It Follows , with the exception of the truly hilarious ball pit and Poltergeist sequences which had me laughing my ass off (I think completely deliberately), which was around the time I was ready to declare everyone straight up wrong about this movie. However it totally faceplants shortly after in the house showdown finale where it devolves entirely into an interminable emotional metaphor movie, the expository explanations of which are among the worst I've seen one of these movies attempt to pull. Shame. When it cuts from the most obvious extramarital affair revelations of all time to the cucked dad(do) holding a gun, followed by Other Mommy punching the annoying Succession guy/cheater's throat and jaw out and exploding his head, I was kinda into this.
