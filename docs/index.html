2026-10-27 Jason X (2001) thejoshl primary 3.0 no Not sure I can intellectually explain or justify why I am far more charmed by the qualities that make the New Line entries in this series "bad" than I am the ones that made the Paramount ones so. I guess maybe it comes down to the fact that I think the flaws of these films stem more from misguided over-ambition and/or deliberate camp rather than simply boring laziness. Regardless, this is somehow the most expensive Friday the 13th movie and yet easily looks the cheapest, in part due to the fact that creature FX artist turned director James Isaac (whose work on The Fly , Naked Lunch and eXistenZ resulted in the delightful Cronenberg cameo) was attempting to take Jason into the world of claustrophobic spaceship horror a la Alien and only had the requisite skill and resources to get to SyFy original Star Trek knock-off. It makes a lot of sense that multiple Canadian cast members went on to be in Andromeda ! Despite this hilariously falling short of what the plan clearly was, there’s an endearing stupidity to it all. Whether it’s the details of how cryogenically frozen lab experiment Jason is brought aboard the ship (after escaping his restraints and impaling Dr. David Cronenberg!), or the nonsensical details of this futuristic Earth II space expedition crew that despite being astronauts/scientists are just as horny and scantily clad as any nubile 80s camp counsellors, or the tonally baffling killing spree setpieces that flip on a dime between being legitimately gross to winking slapstick. Ultimately, I had a good time watching my hulking buddy smash liquid nitrogen heads off countertops, wield a giant machete-sized surgical tool through the chromatic sets, gruesomely spin laser tag armor attired military grunts down corkscrews, fight and have his limbs/head blown off by a sexy dominatrix androids (that dual-wield machine pistols and know kung fu—like Milla in PWSA’s Resident Evil movies), go metallic cyborg “Uber-Jason” mode and get tricked by artificial VR holograms of teen campers going “we love premarital sex!” Him beating them to death in their sleeping bags felt like a return to tradition moment surrounded by all this uncanny modernity. 400 years in the future, it’s very possible we might become civilized enough to ban hockey, but we still be wearing cropped shirts and clamping nipples, and thus we will always need Jason to wake up and put us in our place. Green-screen Kane Hodder weightlessly floating on wires away from CGI explosion in space just a beautifully ugly image I never thought I’d see when I began watching these humble no-budget camp serial killer movies. Also, not generally a fan of writers trying to copycat the Kevin Williamson post-modern Scream dialogue (honestly I barely tolerate him doing it at times), but must confess that “It’s okay guys, he just wanted his machete back!”, “This sucks on so many levels” and “It's gonna take more than a poke in the ribs to put down this old dog… Yeah, that oughta do it...” all got me good. Full discussion coming soon on the podcast .
