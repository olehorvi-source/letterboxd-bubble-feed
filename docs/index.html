2026-10-29 Just Before Dawn (1981) thejoshl primary 4.0 no An extremely atmospheric "slasher" that honestly has more in common with Deliverance or The Hills Have Eyes ; both with its city-slickers being violently ego-checked by nature plot/imagery, and the eerie backwoods hicksploitation physical forces do the dirty work. Huge credit to Lieberman who was tasked at the last minute to retrofit this into something that could compete with Friday the 13th and managed to do so while still making the strange, slow-burn survivalist art version of a horror-thriller he originally intended. Every single time I felt myself getting a bit frustrated at how patient this was being with the obligatory scenes you've seen before (skinny dipping, campfire dancing, etc) it would end up paying off with an incredibly tense and upsetting infiltration by the killer(s) that was more effective precisely because of how quiet and casual the movie goes about it. All the Oregon location work is very beautiful and then somewhere in the periphery of the frame or in the background out of focus suddenly there is this hulking figure getting into the water, or standing at the end of a bridge, or emerging from the tree line and it got me quite literally every single time. (The hand reaching out of the water while she can see her boyfriend at the shoreline + the blurry glasses bits in particular, my god.) Also shoutout to the final girl in this who is given the kind of characterization that suggests she won't make it to long in the great outdoors only to get one of the most uniquely animalistic final confrontations I've ever seen in one of these movies, so much so that it ends up horrifying the supposedly macho boyfriend. Good stuff.
