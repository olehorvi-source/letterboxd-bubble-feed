2026-08-22 Tony (2026) thejoshl primary 3.0 no Matt Johnson's best looking, most normal movie that your parents might even watch with you but at the expense of shaving off a lot of his edges and personality. Say what you will about Blackberry also being a biopic that takes artistic liberty to bend its subject to the autobiographical "man-child who fucks up and learns a lesson through failure" story MJ is attracted to exploring, I wouldn't have mistaken scenes in that movie for 100 other coming-of-age dramedies you've seen come out of Sundance for 30+ years. He hits a number of these beats perfectly pleasantly (and sadly), Bauman gets some really nice photography of Cape Cod, and Matt gets at least two legit terrific periphery performances out of the environment he creates for his actors; Banderas who elevates his religiously strict but genuinely loving mentor role with real weariness, and Woodall who leans into the sad dirtbag quality of his coked-out, womanizing blue-collar chowderhead (not regretting the stock I bought on this guy when I saw Tuner last year). But ultimately, as much as I enjoyed hanging out and laughing with these guys (shoutout Stavvy Baby), and liked how it looked and sounded (great needledrops, good score by Jay), there's a pervasive formulaic plainness to this that felt like an outsider filmmaker trying to prove that they can behave and deliver a conventional product if you were to let them audition for it. Maybe I'd feel differently if this wasn't clearly a for-hire gig about someone he doesn't seem all that interested in (at least I assume, based on how much he changed/remixed to make Bourdain's adolescence more like his own issues with authority and women), and instead he used the same craftspeople and made... idk the actual Matt Johnson biopic and called it Adventureland: Toronto . If you're reading this Matt you can have that pitch for free.
