Occupies an interesting space between being hauntingly ambiguous (maybe even frustratingly, for some) about the details of its literal father-son mystery but ultimately so striking and tactile in terms of its bitter mood and gorgeous landscape photography that the larger symbolism at play here regarding authority and masculinity is all conveyed quite obviously and beautifully despite the ghostly, unresolved nature of the narrative.
