2026-10-15 Eaten Alive (1976) thejoshl primary 4.0 yes Tobe Hooper's underrated follow-up to Texas Chainsaw Massacre which takes on another psychotic redneck serial killer, but this time based on a real-life Texas one who allegedly fed his victims to his swamp alligator and realized with a sense of style that abandons the naturally gritty on-location borderline snuff realism for psychedelically artificial soundstages. Hooper essentially expands on and tries to sustain the more surreal, sweaty, screaming sensory assault finale of TCM for an entire movie and ends up with something closer to an Italian horror sexploitation Psycho . Complete with a deranged Southern bayou motel proprietor Judd (played by Old Hollywood noir actor Neville Brand) murdering a young, on-the-run sex worker who you briefly think might be the protagonist, and then setting his sights on a husband-and-wife (William Finley and Marilyn Burns) who book the next room. On a character/performance/story level you spend most of this in a state of hysterical confusion that suits the unnatural formal choices and glitchy score interspersed throughout all the country-western radio bangers. Effectively doing to your eyes and brain what it's doing to its characters: dropping you into strange, abstract, colorful fog machine swamp that's driving everyone into primal madness and consuming them. Young Robert Englund shows up briefly as the rapist Buck who's "rarin' to fuck" (Tarantino... brother...), the deep Argento red exterior lighting during the scythe kill/dog munching is gorgeous, and all the surreal sex-and-death that follows is unpleasant and hellish as it is in the best Giallos/slashers. Full discussion on ep 116 of my podcast SLEAZOIDS .
