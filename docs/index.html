The Creature from the Black Lagoon -esque eco-horror fishmen monster suits are awesome (I love their exposed brains and seaweed outfits), and the gory deaths the dish out in all the California fishing town locales are even better. The only real sour note is all the rape, which doesn't feel like part of what the movie is about in any way (like say, Alien —a reference point for its final image!) but as repulsively tacked-on in post as it apparently was. Even with Goldblatt keeping things moving along in the edit, it is definitely a little deflating when such an effectively schlocky horror setpiece Peeters atmospherically tees up suddenly turns into a really bad, ugly porn parody of the itself lifelessly directed by some random dude Corman hired to do his dirty work. Despite this, the gruesome waterfront festival massacre final reel is pretty much exactly what you hope for when you turn a movie like this on.
