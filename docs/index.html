The witness protection/hi-tech arms deal paranoid conspiracy set-up (courtesy of The Wild Bunch and Sorcerer co-screenwriter Walon Green + an uncredited John Milius/Frank Darabont??) is as 90s cable techno-thriller as it gets, yet the macho presence of Arnold (and Terminator / Terminator 2 collaborators he brought with him, including cinematographer Adam Greenberg and uncredited screenwriter William Wisher) certainly gives it some of that 80s brand of action sci-fi maximalism too.
