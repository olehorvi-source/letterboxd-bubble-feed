2026-08-26 Sleepaway Camp II: Unhappy Campers (1988) thejoshl primary 2.0 no A much (sigh) campier, tongue-in-cheek follow-up to the original that sees Angela now working as a camp counsellor and graphically murdering naughty campers who break the rules. Not really sure how this characterization makes sense with the previous film but whatever I just went along with it. As a sheer body count slasher this is easily more impressive than the first film as it sees her essentially kill the entire supporting cast in about 75 minutes (often in comically gross ways, including drowning a girl in an outhouse), and honestly Bruce Springsteen's sister Pamela gives a very fun and funny performance as this psychotically prudish virgin counsellor. Where this is obviously worse is in just about everything else... The cast of actual kids being uncomfortably cruel to each other has been replaced by your typical broad horny co-ed archetypes (sometimes for deliberate comedy, other times just laziness), and apologies to whoever directed this but it's one of the flattest looking and sounding movies I've ever seen shot on film. Early on the lack of any sort of mood or tension even during the kills had this uncanny quality that could be mistaken for intentional comedy (I laughed a lot, anyway), but as it went on and the murder shack/woods chase finale tries to get legitimately creepy and suspenseful it just can't do it. This guy simply doesn't know how to shoot or cut a movie. And honestly the rare slasher where the final girl is so far and away the most boring, nothing character I longed for the return of the slutty girl whose two gratuitous sex scenes (the extended one where they struggle to get their clothes of for so long it's almost a ZAZ bit, and the one where she subversively uses the guy and clarifies afterwards that "you don't have AIDS, do you?") are two of the funniest scenes. Still, I laughed a lot: the cut to the camp counsellor looking at the peeping tom polaroid's and casually going "nice tits", the "panty raid" + reverse "jock raid", the Freddy vs. Jason vs. Leatherface bit, the 36 year-old jock teenager who rants about how his cop dad's taxes probably paid for Angela's sex change, which is a character that hasn't aged a day unfortunately. Gonna give this the rare and highly coveted 2 + heart. Leaving room for it to maybe grow on me over time like all the stranger and more baffling Friday the 13th 's have.
