2026-10-01 Primetime (2026) silentdawn secondary 3.0 no 60 Works best as a kaleidoscopic vision of the post-9/11 media circus in all its self-destruction and biblical intensity. You can feel the movie tying all sorts of irreconcilable knots with a flurry of '00s gestures and concerns, including but not limited to: the Hussein Trial, Billy Bob's Sling Blade method act, Rent the musical, Fergie's 'London Bridge', even going so far as framing ABC's What Would You Do? as the hope-core denial of TCAP's justice served. Does it all play? As aesthetic parameters, sure, but something about Lance Oppenheim's non-docu debut kept me at a distance, as if the film itself is constantly torn between embracing its inherent darkness and taking a step back to conceptualize and further define the chaos of the period. Part of this is intentional of course, with the boogeyman even eventually manifesting itself as the ancient evil of generational abuse to haunt Hansen's quest for vengeance (and ratings), although these signifiers rarely coalesce into something more unified or substantial. MVP isn't necessarily Robert Pattinson - who nails Hansen's mannerisms with little room for interiority - but Skyler Gisondo, who carries the film through its particularly thorny passages.
