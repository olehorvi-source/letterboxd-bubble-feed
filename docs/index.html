2026-09-17 The Wolfman (2010) theolejake my_account 3.5 yes I remembered this being sluggish and making very little impression on me when I first watched it about a decade ago. For some reason, though, I completely missed the formal exercises Joe Johnston is whipping up here. It’s an expensive neo-expressionist rendition of the Wolf Man story, which I’ve never thought has been told particularly well in the first place (with the exception of An American Werewolf in London, though that’s kind of doing its own thing). Johnston isn’t able to conjure up much emotion here either, and Benicio del Toro and Emily Blunt leave a lot to be desired in terms of chemistry. But the lavish costumes, production design, and expansive sets create a foggy gothic England that’s more than comforting to spend 100 minutes in. It’s also the first time I can remember the Wolf Man being rendered as a genuinely vicious killing machine in a convincing way. He’s actually menacing when he wolfs out. The early-2010s CGI hasn’t aged particularly well, and Rick Baker’s makeup looks great, even if the design itself is a little goofy.
