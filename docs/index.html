2026-10-13 Whalefallin’ BrianDuffield secondary One of the most difficult yet freeing aspects of making Whalefall was how little we were able to draw on past movies for it. These are a few titles that offered us little sips of water in the desert. A Little Prayer This is one of the great Sundance movies of the last few years and has been something that I evangelize frequently about to anyone within earshot. Jane is just unbelievable in this, as is Straitharn, and similar to Junebug (which the director wrote decades earlier) there is a real sensitivity and reality to the scenes with this family that I’m so struck by, and for me it’s the high bar on family life in recent movies. Inherent Vice My favorite Brolin performance and one of my favorite movies ever. I think Bigfoot is the perfect melting pot of everything Brolin brings to the table as a performer. He can be terrifying one moment and silly and light the next and it all contains within this one complete performance, which I wanted with Mitt - you can see how Austin’s character can feel scared and suffocated by him, and also understand why everyone else adores him. Brokeback Mountain Austin, like everyone, is a big Heath guy, and I remember talking to him about this performance and how you can almost feel how difficult it is for Ennis to get his words out, that there’s this incredible weight and storm inside of him that he’s working so hard to bottle up but you still feel it seeping out of his pores. Jay (outside of the whale) is performing for everyone, and you get these little cracks into seeing this poor guy knows something is wrong with him, and doesn’t have the ability to understand what it is or how to fix himself, until he gets this crazy idea to find his dad’s remains. Patrick and the Whale Just an unbelievably gorgeous doc and probably the narrative piece we went back to the most, that wasn’t the autopsies or endoscopies that for some reason you can’t find on Letterboxd. There’s a stunning sequence where Patrick gets left with a sperm whale calf as a kind of babysitter while its mother hunts, and it’s so beautiful how these two species find a commonality between each other when it comes to family and caring for one another. A must watch, much more enjoyable that whale autopsies and endoscopies I promise. West Side Story A weird reference but I think that’s most of my references, I had a bunch of shots I called “west side story” shots, which usually meant shots that shouldn’t be beautiful or emotional but surprisingly were, which this movie is chock full of. When your movie is inside a whale but also very emotionally forward, you gotta figure something like that out. JFK Putting this here really because Pietro, our editor, cut it, and it’s the best editing a movie will ever have. It really shows you can do anything in the edit as long as you have a narrative conviction, and most of our post was spent grilling Pietro on how he became the GOAT. Cloud Atlas I couldn’t be more of a Wachowski sister fanboy, and this movie is one of their many masterpieces. I thought about it a lot for this because of how completely uncynical or “cool” the emotions are handled. They are handled heart on its sleeve, which I think can be too much for some people to take, but for someone who struggles with emotions in general, sometimes I think I get to unlock parts of myself when filmmakers aren’t afraid to have big open hearts. This movie also has incredible cross cutting between very different scenes that begin working together, and I thought of that a lot in our more big swing third act.
